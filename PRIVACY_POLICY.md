# Privacy Policy for Workout Timer

**Last updated: January 2026**

## Introduction

Workout Timer ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Workout Timer stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- **Workout Settings**: Work time, rest time, and number of rounds preferences
- **Workout Presets**: Custom workout configurations you create
- **Workout History**: Records of your completed workouts (last 50 sessions)
- **Heart Rate Data** (Wear OS only): Heart rate measurements during workouts

**This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers.**

### Permissions We Request

| Permission | Purpose | Required |
|------------|---------|----------|
| **Vibration** | Haptic feedback during workouts | Yes |
| **Wake Lock** | Keep screen on during active workouts | Yes |
| **Body Sensors** (Wear OS) | Heart rate monitoring during workouts | Optional |
| **Foreground Service** | Continue timer when app is minimized | Yes |
| **Notifications** | Workout alerts and reminders | Optional |

## Data We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Any data to third-party services
- Payment information

## Data Storage & Security

- All data is stored locally using secure device storage (AsyncStorage for mobile, DataStore for Wear OS)
- No cloud backup or synchronization
- You can delete all stored data through the app's "Clear History" feature
- Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

## Third-Party Services

Workout Timer does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Workout Timer does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences interested in fitness.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be noted with a new "Last updated" date.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your stored workout data (visible in-app)
- Delete your data at any time (via "Clear History")
- Uninstall the app to remove all data
